MyHomeNest privacy policy

MyHomeNest is used only by its owner to control their own Nest thermostat through Home Assistant running on their home network.

  • Thermostat data (temperature, humidity, and settings) is sent from Google directly to the owner's Home Assistant and stays on that home system.
  • No data is collected, stored, sold, or shared with anyone else.
  • No other users can sign in to or use this app.
